Hi! My name is Catherine, but you can call me Cat. I'm a junior student at the University of Illinois Chicago. I went in as a Computer Science major, but in Fall 2025, I took my first Earth and Environmental Science class. I enjoyed it so much that I decided to add it as another major and as a result discovered I care about solving environmental problems with technical systems! I've always appreciated collaborative environments and working with other people for a common goal.

This is part of the reason why I love hackathons so much. Over the past two years, I've attended five hackathons: HackMIT 2024, FitchGroup Codeathon 2024, CivicHacks 2025, SparkHacks 2025, HackHarvard 2025, and SparkHacks 2026! I've had great success as a project manager--breaking up conflicts, dividing up work, taking an active role in presenting (I was an actor in high school and as a result very much so enjoy public speaking), and making Figma prototypes-- but I've recently moved into more front-end roles at hackathons. I tried using Node.js and React for the first time and I loved it! Using the skills I learned at hackathons has enabled me to succeed in my Software Design class, which is often very front-end heavy.

Outside of hackathons, I've taken an active role in CS clubs. Since fall 2025, I've been the Special Interest Group (SIG) Hacks lead. During weekly club meetings, I help my students learn vital skills such as how to brainstorm, how to prepare and apply for hackathons, how to debrief after hackathons, and learn essential skills such as using DevPost. As of spring 2026 I have also become a Women in Computer Science mentor. Every three weeks, I meet with my mentee for one hour so that I can provide advice on how to balance project-heavy CS classes, how to write a good resume, how to navigate the process of selecting classes, and how to strengthen programming skills as a CS student. I feel very lucky that I am able to step into these leadership roles and seeing my students make very interesting projects during hackathons or my mentee succeeding at a difficult project fills me with elation.

Outside of UIC, I want to bring my skills to a Chicago-based company that's focused on sustainability. I am particularly interested in software engineering roles and frontend roles in a collaborative environment. 

Programming Languages:  C++, C, Java
Programming Software and Frameworks: React, Git, VS Code, IntelliJ, Node.js
Relevant coursework: Data Structures, Software Design, Computer Hardware

Pivot Point-- created for SparkHacks at UIC in 2026. Our project is primarily frontend based, with the skeleton of the project being a decision tree. CSS was utilized to create the background imagery of each text slide and orient the font and text boxes. We utilized React in order to provide punchy scenarios and their relevant answers. Clicking on an answer takes you to another scenario that comprises the consequences of the previous answer. To spark ideas on possible paths, we created a Figma with sample scenarios and a large decision tree of actions and consequences to see how everything interconnected. From that, we chose the most interesting actions and consequences and built a React and CSS file for each question and action slide.
https://github.com/CatherineMarkowska/PivotPoint
